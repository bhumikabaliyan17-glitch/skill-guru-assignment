# The Skill Guru Foundation Assignment

## Your Understanding

The Skill Guru Foundation is an organization focused on skill development and education. They help students and professionals learn new skills, especially in digital marketing, content creation, and social media.

## Creativity Task - 2 Ideas

**Idea 1: Skill Spotlight Series** - Weekly 30-second Reels showing one in-demand skill with a quick tip.

**Idea 2: Student Takeover Day** - Let a student run Instagram Stories for 4-5 hours sharing their learning journey.
