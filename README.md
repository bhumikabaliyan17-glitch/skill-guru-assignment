# skill-guru-assignment
Assignment for The Skill Guru Foundation - Content and Social Media Internship role

#The Skill Guru Foundation
is an organization focused on skill development and education. They help students and professionals learn new skills, especially in digital marketing, content creation, and social media. They aim to bridge the gap between education and industry requirements by providing practical training and guidance.
#ideas related to content and social media
Create a weekly 30-second video series where we highlight one in-demand skill (e.g., Canva, Excel, ChatGPT, Prompt Engineering). Each video will show:
- What the skill is
- Why it's useful
- A quick tip or trick

This will attract students and young professionals who want to upskill quickly. Hashtags: #SkillSpotlight #LearnFast #SkillGuru

#Student Takeover Day" (Instagram Stories)
Once a week, let a student or intern take over the Instagram account for 4-5 hours. They will share:
- What they are learning
- A small challenge they solved
- Behind-the-scenes of their internship

This builds trust, shows real learning experience, and encourages more students to join. It also creates user-generated content for free.
